About Us

Your Trusted Transportation Partner for Business in Jakarta

PT Amarta Prima Persada (APP) is a provider of corporate car rental services in Jakarta and online driver partner services for Grab and Gojek. We offer a complete Toyota fleet (Avanza, Veloz, Innova Reborn, to Innova Zenix Hybrid) for your company's operational needs.

Strategically located in Central Jakarta, we serve the Greater Jakarta area (Jakarta, Tangerang, Bekasi, Depok, and Bogor). With a well-maintained fleet, complete legal documents, and 24-hour professional service, we help companies and individuals run their operations efficiently, safely, and without the hassle of vehicle ownership.
